## District Expansion System

### Land Reclamation Pipeline
- Contaminated wasteland plots revealed by recon sweeps or events
- Player orders reclamation/cleanup on discovered plots
- Contamination grades affect cleanup time:
  - Light contamination: ~2-3 weeks
  - Heavy/toxic: ~1-2 months
  - Nuclear: ~2-3 months
- Costs credits, steel, and resources
- More waste processing/reclamation buildings = faster cleanup

### District Construction on Cleared Plots
- Cleared plots enter a "ready" pool
- Player commissions new district construction (~1-3 months)
- New districts start empty, require full buildout (power, water, housing, etc.)
- Total timeline from discovery to functioning district: ~2-4 months

### Auto-Develop Mode
- Toggle on cleared plots to automatically queue district construction
- System uses available resources; pauses if supplies run dry
- Idle-friendly: city slowly expands for players who check in periodically

### Annexation Through Diplomacy
- High influence/loyalty with factions or settlements unlocks annexation option
- Hostile factions require military pressure first
- Annexed territory adds new districts with pre-existing (possibly damaged) infrastructure

### Wasteland Expansion (Recon-Driven)
- Recon sweeps discover unclaimed wasteland sectors
- Player commissions mega-infrastructure contract to reclaim the zone
- Expensive, slow, rewarding

### Population Pressure Events
- Overcrowding triggers expansion events
- Player chooses: build vertically (upgrade existing) or expand outward (new districts)

### Contract-Based District Unlocks
- Certain mega-scale infrastructure contracts reward new district slots on completion

### Event-Driven District Additions
- Refugee caravans establish shanty districts (unplanned, need cleanup/upgrade)
- Corporate buyouts absorb neighboring sectors
- Faction peace deals include territory transfers
- Surprise additions the player must adapt to

## War Room Overhaul

### Military Asset Overview
- Full stats dashboard: units, vehicles, weapons, missiles, armaments, ammo
- Complete overview of all military assets in one place
- At-a-glance readiness, strength, and supply levels

### Standing Army Management
- Maintain a permanent standing army drawn from your unit pool
- Add/remove troops, vehicles, drones, and equipment to your army roster
- Army composition affects defense rating, deterrence, and strike capability
- Draw from your standing army for missions (faction assaults, megacity sieges, wasteland ops)
- Depleted army units must be replenished through recruitment

### Military Production Chains
- Weapons foundries, ammunition plants, missile assembly facilities
- Research chains for advanced military tech (railguns, plasma weapons, orbital strike capability)
- Resource pipelines: raw materials → components → finished armaments
- Production speed tied to industrial capacity and power supply

### Military Policies
- Conscription policies (voluntary, mandatory, emergency draft)
- Rules of engagement settings (restrained, standard, unrestricted)
- Military budget allocation
- Veterans affairs and wounded soldier programs
- Martial law protocols

### Military Research
- Armor systems, weapons platforms, tactical AI
- Drone swarm technology, autonomous combat systems
- Siege equipment, fortification engineering
- Nuclear/biological deterrent programs (consequences guaranteed)

## Projects & Research Chains

Megastructure projects with 5-step research chains. Each project is unique (one per city), has meaningful trade-offs, and generates positive/negative events.

### 2. Civic Command Nexus
- **Purpose**: Centralized city control and coordination
- **Research Chain**: Municipal Data Integration → Real-Time Operations Monitoring → Cross-Department Protocol Systems → Predictive Incident Modeling → Autonomous Command Algorithms
- **Bonuses**: +Global efficiency (all systems), faster emergency response, reduced infrastructure downtime
- **Penalties**: High power consumption, vulnerable single-point failure
- **Events**:
  - ✅ System Synchronization Success → temporary boost to all outputs
  - ❌ Command Overload → cascading delays across city systems

### 3. Population Regulation Bureau
- **Purpose**: Control migration, density, and housing
- **Research Chain**: Census Automation Systems → Residency Tracking Networks → Migration Pattern Analysis → Density Optimization Models → Behavioral Compliance Incentives
- **Bonuses**: Stabilized population growth, reduced homelessness, better workforce distribution
- **Penalties**: Reduced citizen happiness, increased unrest risk if overused
- **Events**:
  - ✅ Efficient Relocation Program → housing demand drops
