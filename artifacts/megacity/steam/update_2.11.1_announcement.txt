MEGACITY: Sector Marshal — Update 2.11.1: Peace Means Peace

Version: 2.11.1
Build: 25
Released: 2026-07-13
Save compatibility: Existing saves load as-is — nothing needs resetting.

Wars now end properly, prosperity has standards, and two new residential-tower stories are waiting. The city remains ungrateful.

---

THE HEADLINE: CEASEFIRES THAT HOLD

- Accept or counter-propose a ceasefire and you’ll get a confirmation dispatch and news-ticker update.
- The aggressor actually stands down instead of immediately reopening the same conflict.
- Duplicate offers and repeat-reward exploits are gone, with older saves cleaned up automatically.

TWO NEW STORYLINES

- THE QUIET FLOORS and THE RED DOOR are once-per-playthrough branching stories set in the residential towers. Each has eight chapters, with endings shaped by your choices.

A CITY AT WAR FEELS IT

- Twelve new war-only events bring false air-raid alarms, overcrowded field hospitals, broadcast hijacks, missing conscripts, and other emergencies. Each offers three responses.

THE GOLDEN AGE HAS STANDARDS

- Prosperity now requires decent housing and a healthy environment alongside wealth and morale. A strong balance sheet cannot hide squalor.
- A one-time advisory and news-ticker hint identify the blocker when housing or ecology keeps the Golden Age out of reach.

ALSO NEW

- Collapse the Active Incidents list on the CITY tab; a badge still shows how many need attention.
- Officer actions now show their results, stat changes, and credits spent, with protection against accidental double-taps.

FIXED

- Administration → Systems upgrades now apply their advertised tax, trade, utility, crime, unrest, health, and research effects.
- Story rewards, including orbital-elevator trade income and research bonuses, now reach the budget and research system.
- News-linked dispatches now show the correct in-game date.

NOTES

- Existing saves are fully compatible. No new city required — every change above applies to your current save.
