## 2. Gameplay feature details (everything in the trailer is real)

The hook that separates it from every other city game:
> **This is not a city builder. The city already exists. Your job is to stop it from killing itself.**

You are the **Sector Marshal** of a post-collapse megacity — part mayor, part warlord,
part bureaucrat. Core facts the trailer leans on (all from the live game):

- **The city:** ~**980,000 citizens**, **268 districts**, **9 resources** (Credits, Food,
  Water, Power, Steel, Goods, Fuel, Medical Supplies, Ammunition). If one hits zero,
  something terrible happens. If several do, something *cinematic* happens.
- **Tick-based & idle-friendly:** runs in real time (default 5 min/tick, 1–60
  configurable), simulates while you're away with offline catch-up (Lite / Standard /
  Deep). Auto-save, Steam Cloud syncs saves *and* commander profiles.
- **Factions:** **10 rival factions (+2 hidden)** with leaders and intel dossiers, plus
  **13 external megacities/nations** to trade with, spy on, or destabilize.
- **Crime & enforcement:** **134 crime types**, **53 named gangs** with dossiers; deploy
  judges, soldiers, enforcers, riot mechs — or don't.
- **Governance:** **77 edicts**, **260 policies**, **100 spy operations**.
- **Research & industry:** **520+ technologies**, **836 commodities** on a dynamic
  market, **303 weapons**, **100 licensed corporations**.
- **Events & comedy:** **1,150+ events / 745 multi-stage chains**, plus a **350+
  satirical news ticker**. (The vending machine that filed for union representation; the
  pigeons that remember everything; the therapy bot that achieved sentience and
  immediately requested therapy.)
- **Faith & the Leader Cult:** 3 street faiths — Sponsor, Tolerate, or Suppress — and if
  one dominates you can **declare yourself its Leader/god** (power + serious blowback).
- **Your commander:** an RPG layer — 5 attributes, 12 skills, perks, dynamic titles,
  **112 cybernetic implants**, reputation tracked across 5 axes.
- **End states (no fixed win screen):** ACTIVE · CITY FALLEN · MACHINE ASCENSION ·
  BIOLOGICAL PERPETUATION. Survive, ascend, or burn out spectacularly.
- **Player-friendly:** **Free to play. No microtransactions. No loot boxes. No battle
  passes.** Solo-developed.

## 6. How to use this with invideo AI (important)

invideo AI builds videos from **stock footage + AI voiceover + AI music + captions**. It
does **not** have MEGACITY's gameplay. So pick a workflow:

- **Workflow A — real gameplay feature trailer (recommended).** Record your gameplay first
  (Section 7). In invideo, generate the project from the prompt in Section 5, then open the
  media/asset panel, **upload your clips**, and replace the stock clip in each `[GAMEPLAY]`
  scene with your matching recording. Keep invideo's AI voiceover, music, captions, and
  timing. This is the only way to get a trailer that actually shows the game.
- **Workflow B — fast concept/announce trailer.** Let invideo auto-fill every scene with
  stock footage. Quick, but it shows generic sci-fi cities, not your game — fine for a
  teaser, weak as a *feature* trailer.

**Recording tips:** capture at 1920×1080, 60fps if you can. Use **Pause (Space)** for clean
framing and **Photo Mode (H)** for cinematic shots. Grab ~4–6 seconds per screen so invideo
has room to trim to the beat. If invideo's AI voice isn't gritty enough, record the Section
4 lines yourself (or in a TTS tool) and upload them as the voiceover.
