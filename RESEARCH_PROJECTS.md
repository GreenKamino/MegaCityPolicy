# MEGACITY — Research Projects Compendium

This document lists every research project available in **MEGACITY**. It covers the full standard research catalog — **714 projects** spanning **32 categories** and tiers 1–5 — followed by a separate section on **successor technologies** that are unlocked through narrative event chains rather than standard research.

Each entry lists its category, tier, research cost (in research points), prerequisites (by name), a short description, and a plain-language summary of its effects on the city.
